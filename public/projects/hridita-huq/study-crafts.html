Made slowly, detail by detail

The human
touch.

A stitch, a fold, a carefully placed bead. Handwork invites us to slow down and see the gestures that give a surface its character.

↘ Choose a detail to explore

Observation 01

A line made with thread.

A sequence of small stitches can become an outline, a stem, or a field of texture. Repetition gives the surface its rhythm.

↳Follow a stitched line as it changes direction.

Observation 02

A detail that catches the light.

Raised details add another layer to the cloth. Their placement can create a focal point or quietly echo the shape of a motif.

↳Look for the small highlights among the embroidered shapes.

Observation 03

Care at the edges.

Finishing draws attention to the smallest parts of a garment: an edge, a fold, or the meeting of two surfaces. These details complete the story.
