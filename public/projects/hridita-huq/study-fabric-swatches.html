Studio journal / Fabric swatchesChapter 03 — 07

Texture, weight & possibility

A feeling
for fabric.

Before a material becomes a garment, there is the pleasure of looking closely. Explore four textures and imagine how each could change a silhouette.

↘ Choose a detail to explore

Observation 01

An open, tactile surface.

The visible weave and softly frayed edge give this sample a relaxed character. Its texture becomes part of the design.

↳Look at the tiny spaces between the threads.

Observation 02

Depth in a darker tone.

The dense, nubbled surface makes the cocoa sample feel substantial in the composition. It provides contrast beside lighter layers.

↳Notice how the surface catches small pockets of shadow.

Observation 03

Softness in the folds.

Gentle highlights follow the rose sample as it folds. That change between light and shadow helps describe its movement.

↳Trace the light along the edge of a fold.

Observation 04

A little light passing through.

The pale translucent layer lets the tones beneath remain visible. Overlapping it changes both the colour and the sense of depth.

↳Compare a single layer with an overlapping fold.
