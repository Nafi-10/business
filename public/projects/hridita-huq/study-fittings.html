Studio journal / FittingsChapter 05 — 07

Pin, adjust & look again

A conversation
with form.

Fitting is a moment to pause and observe. A small change can alter the balance, comfort, and expression of the whole silhouette.

↘ Choose a detail to explore

Observation 01

Read the whole silhouette.

Start with the overall relationship of shoulder, waist, and hem. Look for areas where the cloth rests naturally or appears to pull.

↳Observe the garment before focusing on a single seam.

Observation 02

Let a pin suggest the change.

Pinning holds a possible adjustment long enough to assess it. A temporary change can reveal a different line without making it final.

↳Look at the cloth gathering around the pinned area.

Observation 03

Keep the conversation open.

An adjustment is a proposal. Looking again from another angle helps decide whether it supports the intention of the design.

↳Imagine the same form from the side and the back.
