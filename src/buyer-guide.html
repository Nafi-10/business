Ready-made software

THE EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS
Am I buying access, a download or source code?

These are different deliverables. StockFlow is currently a hosted-software concept: planned account access does not imply a source-code purchase. Check the individual product’s delivery format and licence before ordering.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. The current catalogue contains previews and illustrative prices. Confirm the billing period, user limits, setup fees, hosting, taxes where applicable and cancellation terms before making a purchase. This preview does not take payments.

Will it work with my devices and existing data?

Check supported browsers, devices, connectivity and any required integrations. If you have existing records, discuss their format, migration scope and responsibilities before choosing a solution.

What should I confirm about updates and support?

Ask which updates are included, how to request help, the agreed support hours and whether onboarding is included. Refund eligibility, renewal and cancellation terms should be provided for the specific product before purchase.

UI/UX design

FROM INTERFACE TO EXPERIENCE
Does a design kit include a working website?

A UI kit contains design assets; it is not automatically a coded website or app. Custom UI/UX work can cover research, flows, screens and prototypes. Development, hosting and deployment need their own agreed scope.

Which files and tools will I receive?

The Commerce UI Kit is currently a visual preview, with editable Figma files planned. Before ordering, confirm the exact files, components, responsive layouts, fonts, plugins and developer handover materials included.

Can I use the design for a client or commercial project?

Check the specific licence for commercial use, client work, project limits and redistribution. Fonts, photographs and other third-party assets may have separate terms. These rights are not finalized for the current preview.

Can the design be tailored to my brand?

Yes, you can discuss a custom UI/UX project. Share your audience, brand assets, required screens and reference ideas so the proposal can define deliverables, revision rounds, milestones and handover.

Custom ERP systems

BUILT AROUND YOUR BUSINESS
How is the scope and price decided?

Start with your industry, users, processes and required modules. The proposal should define milestones, integrations, migration, deployment and acceptance criteria. The ERP configurator is a discussion aid, not a binding quote.

Can it connect to my existing systems?

Connections depend on the systems involved and the access they provide. Share your current software, APIs and data formats for assessment. Integration availability and migration effort must be confirmed during scoping.

Who owns the system, source code and business data?

These responsibilities belong in the project agreement. Confirm ownership, source-code access, administrator access, data export, hosting arrangements and third-party licences before development starts.

What happens after the system is launched?

Agree training, documentation, maintenance, backup responsibilities and a support channel as part of the scope. New features and changes outside that scope should be estimated separately, with delivery expectations agreed in advance.

AI & chatbots

HELPFUL CONVERSATIONS, BY DESIGN
Is the chatbot preview connected to live AI?

No. Assist AI is currently a scripted interaction preview using sample answers. A deployed chatbot needs an agreed knowledge source, provider, channel and integrations; the preview does not demonstrate live order or account access.

Can a bot answer questions about my business?

A project can be scoped around content you approve, such as product information and FAQs. Agree how that content is updated, what the bot should avoid answering and when it should hand a conversation to a person.

What costs and usage limits should I check?

Confirm setup fees, included messages, model or provider charges, channel fees and maintenance. The illustrative subscription price does not establish final usage allowances or include every possible third-party cost.

How will customer information be handled?

Before launch, agree what information is collected, who can access it, where providers process it, how long it is kept and how deletion requests are handled. Only connect business data and actions that the project explicitly needs.
