Studio journal / SketchesChapter 01 — 07

An idea, finding its line

Before the
first stitch.

A sketch leaves room for possibility. Follow the lines, explore the proportions, and see how a first thought begins to take shape.

↘ Choose a detail to explore

Observation 01

Start with the outline.

The outer line gives a garment its first impression. Notice the long shapes and the space around the body.

↳Follow the line from shoulder to hem.

Observation 02

Let the marks move.

Loose, repeated pencil strokes suggest a fold, a turn, or the direction of falling cloth. The drawing can stay open to change.

↳Look for the diagonal sweeps of fabric.

Observation 03

Change one relationship.

A higher waist or a longer skirt changes the balance of a look. Sketching makes it possible to compare ideas before committing to one.

↳Compare the upper and lower parts of each figure.
